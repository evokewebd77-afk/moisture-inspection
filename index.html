Frequently asked questions

Questions About Moisture Inspections and Leak Detection

Is every damp patch caused by a leaking pipe?

A description or photograph alone cannot confirm the cause. Explain when and where the dampness appears so the appropriate type of investigation can be discussed.

Who carries out specialist leak detection?

FreshGuard works with BA-MAH Totaaltechniek for specialist leak detection. Confirm the attending provider, quotation and agreed scope when arranging the service.

Will walls or floors need to be opened?

Specialist methods may help investigate without unnecessary opening of surfaces. The access required depends on the property and the findings. Discuss any opening work with the provider before it proceeds.

Does thermal imaging show every hidden leak?

No single inspection method can answer every question. Discuss what the proposed inspection can establish, its limitations and whether further checks may be needed.

Does the inspection include repairs?

Do not assume repairs are included. Ask for a clear distinction between investigation, reporting and any separately arranged repair or restoration work.

Can I use the report for an insurance claim?

Ask about the documentation available before booking and check your insurer's requirements. An inspection or report does not guarantee reimbursement or claim acceptance.

How much will the inspection cost?

Request a quotation for the relevant investigation. Confirm attendance charges, the included work, reporting and any additional fees before approving the appointment.
